## Why R305 exists

The accumulated OMEGA design ledger has repeated one invariant across generations: preserve the full machine while pruning duplication, and do not let growth bury working functions. The recovered one-system ledger requires no orphan feature; later renderer/system ledgers require no dead menu and no orphan route. R107 already corrected the historical 44-route count into inventory telemetry rather than architecture, but the real-browser R286 proof still froze that historical count in several assertions.

R305 converts the no-burial rule into a cross-ledger machine check and removes that route-count ceiling.
