# R457 · OMEGA7 Heightened Mode Composition Law

R457 is the developmental law for continuing OMEGA7 after accepted 44/44 product parity and the R455 structured-receipt crash repair.

It does not replace the accepted product. It defines how future development may add depth without collapsing capability identity, composition logic, presentation, proof authority, or rollback into one layer.

## The law

OMEGA7 development is separated into three roles:

```
CAPABILITY
    ↓
COMPOSITION
    ↓
PRESENTATION
```

### Capability

Capability is what the system can lawfully do.

A capability contract retains:

- stable capability identity;
- accepted OMEGAv6 route identity;
- execution input;
- execution output;
- execution proof;
- current reality/availability boundary;
- OMEGA layer participation;
- evidence class.

Presentation cannot redefine those properties.

### Composition

Composition is how multiple preserved capabilities are combined for a task.

A composition retains:

- native family identity;
- source boundary;
- failure-isolation proof;
- performance proof;
- rollback proof;
- unselected sibling capabilities as future topology.

Selecting two capabilities in a six-capability family does not delete or normalize away the other four.

R457 therefore treats composition as **bounded selection over a preserved future topology**, not as destructive flattening.

### Presentation

Presentation is the human projection of capability and composition.

It may change:

- label;
- grouping;
- human domain;
- visible density;
- Standard / Advanced / Canon depth;
- progressive disclosure.

It may not change:

- capability identity;
- execution authority;
- evidence authority;
- proof class;
- CanonState;
- accepted parity;
- rollback.

Presentation authority is therefore explicitly:

`PRESENTATION_ONLY`

## Contradictions and scars

R457 carries the developmental rule:

`RETAIN_DO_NOT_NORMALIZE_AWAY`

A contradiction, rejected branch, failure receipt, or migration scar remains part of the future decision state.

A cleaner UI is not permission to delete negative knowledge.

## Future-topology-aware pruning

OMEGA7 may simplify **presentation**, but capability retirement is a different operation.

R457 requires:

`EXPLICIT_PARITY_AND_REPLACEMENT_PROOF_REQUIRED`

and still returns `HOLD` for retirement requests.

The rule is intentionally conservative: accepted capability remains present until a later governed revision proves replacement coverage, future-path preservation, rollback, and authority equivalence.

R457 itself retires nothing.

## Scientific boundary

R457 introduces no new physical primitive.

The atlas ladder remains an address/resolution system.

`physicalDimensionClaim:false`

## Practical consequence

Future OMEGA7 work should answer three separate questions:

1. **Capability:** what exact lawful function already exists or is being added?
2. **Composition:** how does it combine with preserved functions without destroying future paths?
3. **Presentation:** what is the clearest human view of that composition at the selected depth?

This is the successor rule for making OMEGA7 simpler to use while increasing rather than reducing its technical depth.
