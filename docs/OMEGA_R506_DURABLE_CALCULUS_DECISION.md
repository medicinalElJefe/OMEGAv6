# OMEGA R506 — Durable Calculus Decision Ledger

R506 closes the continuity gap left after R505: an autonomous reasoning worker could prove that it applied the calculus, but the accepted decision was not itself carried forward as durable state evidence.

Every accepted R314/R388 reasoning mutation now carries an R506 decision record into the candidate receipt. The record binds the exact calculus context, applied operators, considered alternatives, selected alternative, STAY/TURN/ESCALATE decision, rationale, residual evidence, predicted eight-field developmental delta and its ΔΩ score.

The initial result condition is deliberately `PREDICTED_PENDING_RETURNED_PROOF`. A positive ΔΩ remains a prediction, never proof. Returned CI/runtime/deployment/Hybrid/browser evidence must later determine the observed outcome and calibration error.

R506 adds no Canon admission, source-promotion or production authority. R125, R142, R164, R240 and `ci.yml` remain unchanged.

This establishes the evidence structure required for the next developmental step: prediction → execution → returned proof → observed delta → calibration scar → next-cycle worker context.
