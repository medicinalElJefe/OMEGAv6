# R509 · Falsifiable Capability Contracts

R509 turns acceptance-contract debt into explicit capability contracts without weakening R507/R508 finite convergence.

## Core law

A convergence item is not autonomous work merely because source files exist.

Each item must first carry a qualified contract with:

- exact item identity and exact objective text;
- an explicit admission lane;
- exact source surfaces;
- independent proof families;
- positive evidence predicates;
- explicit falsifiers;
- a truth boundary;
- explicit source-mutation authority, which defaults to false.

The R509 lanes are:

1. BOUNDED_SOURCE — a semantically bounded product-source transition is authorized.
2. CURRENT_SOURCE_PROOF — exact current source may already satisfy the objective; OMEGA may generate a proof/state candidate only.
3. LIVE_BROWSER_PROOF — current product behavior must be measured through an exact-head browser proof; source mutation is not implied.
4. LIVE_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE — provider-returned evidence is required; source mutation cannot substitute for unavailable evidence.
5. HYBRID_HOST_EVIDENCE — authenticated host execution and returned artifacts are required; cloud/source claims cannot substitute for host proof.

Only BOUNDED_SOURCE contributes product mutation budget.

CURRENT_SOURCE_PROOF contributes proof action budget but zero mutation budget.

LIVE_BROWSER_PROOF, LIVE_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE and HYBRID_HOST_EVIDENCE remain visible qualified debt until their specified evidence returns.

## Contract epochs

A decline scar is evidence, but its scheduling authority is scoped to the acceptance contract that produced it.

- A decline recorded under the same contract revision holds another attempt.
- A decline from an older explicit revision is preserved as history but does not veto a newer qualified contract.
- A legacy pre-contract decline remains active unless the new contract explicitly sets `supersedesLegacyDeclines:true`.
- That supersession creates one bounded new evidence epoch; a new decline under the same revision holds again.

B-04 is the first migrated example. Its R509 contract explicitly supersedes the old pre-contract decline and enters CURRENT_SOURCE_PROOF, not UI mutation. Exact source must prove the shared AUTO/DESKTOP/MOBILE frame and bounded desktop/mobile navigation geometry; exact-head R241 still decides rendered parity.

## First systematic qualification batch: SAR / remote sensing

R509 explicitly qualifies the remaining D-section obligations:

- D-02 native/complex SAR acquisition: LIVE_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE.
- D-03 calibrated/interferometric/unwrapped/LOS products: HYBRID_HOST_EVIDENCE.
- D-04 corrected deformation after correction obligations: HYBRID_HOST_EVIDENCE.
- D-05 multi-geometry 3-D veto/admission law: CURRENT_SOURCE_PROOF.
- D-06 field-first unobstructed SAR composition: CURRENT_SOURCE_PROOF plus exact-head R241/R202.
- D-07 non-text visual/semantic lens distinguishability: LIVE_BROWSER_PROOF.

D-02 through D-04 may not generate product repair merely because provider/host evidence is absent.

D-05 and D-06 may re-enter autonomous work only through the R507 proof-only path. If exact source does not satisfy their contract, they do not fall through into AI mutation.

D-07 remains open until a browser proof measures rendered visual signatures rather than reading labels or prose.

## Finite-convergence interaction

R509 separates:

- mutationBudget;
- proofBudget;
- actionBudget = mutationBudget + proofBudget.

A proof-only candidate can therefore proceed even when mutationBudget is zero.

When actionBudget reaches zero, the current evidence epoch is quiescent. New work may enter only through new evidence, a newly qualified contract, or a genuinely different bounded repair hypothesis.

## Truth boundary

Contract qualification is not capability completion.

A contract says what would falsify or establish a capability and which authority lane may act. Completion still requires the contract's evidence and exact-head proof.

R125 remains sole CanonState admission authority. ci.yml remains sole canonical production writer. R509 adds no physical primitives, no scientific truth by declaration, and no autonomous authority expansion.
