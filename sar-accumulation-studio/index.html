Truth semantics

Derived. Registered stack statistics, QA masks, Canon host variables, and signed slant-range change retain exact source manifests and never become new observations.

Contextual / inferred. NASA imagery, Earth proxy cells, and Atlas gap estimates are never promoted into SAR measurements.
