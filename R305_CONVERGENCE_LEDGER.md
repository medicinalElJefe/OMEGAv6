## R305 purpose

R305 closes two related presentation/proof failure classes without creating a second router, a second Canon writer, or a new execution authority:

1. **Interaction authority** — a visible control must actually own its interaction geometry on desktop and coarse-pointer mobile. Center-point hit testing, minimum touch geometry, reduced-motion behavior, viewport containment, and active-workspace occlusion are browser-proven.
2. **Capability reachability** — a registered/recovered function must remain discoverable through the one current route authority. The audit correlates application routes, shared navigation, recovered master menus, recovered software systems/menu options/capabilities, the eight functional layers, and mode/calculus authority telemetry.

The combined rule is:

> **NO_LAYER_MAY_BURY_A_REGISTERED_FUNCTION**

This means both semantic reachability and physical interaction reachability are release concerns.
